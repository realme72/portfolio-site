About me; the basics

I'm a software engineer who builds scalable distributed systems for e-commerce and payments.

Over 4+ years at Tata 1mg, Morgan Stanley and Wizcommerce, I've shipped systems across B2B and B2C commerce, payment workflows and third-party integrations. That ranges from health partners like eKincare and MediBuddy at 1mg to payment gateways like Revolut Pay at Wizcommerce, each built on clear, versioned contracts so partners plug in safely and failures stay contained.

What gets me going

Software that gets easier to change as it grows, not harder.

I lean on design patterns and SOLID principles to shape systems so the next feature is a small, safe addition instead of a rewrite. When I rebuilt our payment gateway layer around Strategy and Registry patterns, adding a new gateway went from touching core logic to dropping in a plug-in.

I also design for the failures nobody sees until 3 a.m.: duplicate webhooks, retries that could charge twice, tenants racing for the same order number. Idempotency, state machines and event-driven flows keep things correct when networks and providers don't cooperate.

Lately I'm bringing that same discipline to LLMs, RAG and vector search, and shipping my own products, like ResumeSmith.

Where I've worked

I've spent the last 4+ years building scalable distributed systems across B2B and B2C e-commerce and payments, at Wizcommerce, Morgan Stanley (via Teamware Solutions) and Tata 1mg.

Along the way I've owned the integrations that businesses depend on: payment gateways like Revolut Pay and health partners like eKincare and MediBuddy, each built on clear contracts so partners plug in cleanly and failures stay contained. I also designed an SMS notification service with delivery integration and status updates, built on Amazon SQS to handle high-concurrency load.
